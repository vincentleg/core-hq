## Not asserted

No current user count, revenue, funding, partners, customers, incorporation, security certification, independent audit, calibrated digital twin, production city deployment, or measured improvement is claimed. CORE Business and CORE Cities are directions rather than commercial deployments. Broader domains and global scale are ambitions.
